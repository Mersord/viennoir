A legend – from a new perspective.

Five women. One legend. A file opens.

Ladies of Bond enters a world of glamour, danger and iconic songs. This time, however, the perspective belongs to the women: the characters who challenge, seduce, rescue or have long since seen through Bond.

Live vocals, dance, burlesque and aerial artistry merge into a cinematic cabaret thriller. Elegant, self-assured and told with a wink, the show completely reimagines a familiar legend.
