The story

Where desire meets Viennese society.

A Viennese scandal – Bridgerton inspired.

In a society where every word is a weapon and every touch a promise, a love triangle ignites. A lavish ball becomes the stage for intrigue, longing and decisions that no one can undo.

Pop meets classical music, powerful voices meet aerial acrobatics, and opulent costumes meet Viennese theatricality. Season of Desire is romantic, sensual and dramatic – with a modern pulse beneath its historical surface.

Season of Desire · FAQ

Common questions about the Bridgerton-inspired show Season of Desire

What is “Season of Desire – A Viennese Scandal”?

“Season of Desire” is a Bridgerton-inspired stage show by Vien.noir at Das Vindobona in Vienna. At its heart is Lady Arabella Fairmont, who must choose between desire and duty during the Viennese social season – a ball, a love triangle and a society where every smile conceals an intention. The production combines live vocals, aerial acrobatics and sensual theatricality, with pop meeting classical music: the atmosphere of Bridgerton, told with a distinctly Viennese touch.

Is Season of Desire a Bridgerton show?

Season of Desire is inspired by Bridgerton, but it is neither an official Netflix production nor a re-enactment of the series. We tell an original story in the same world of ballrooms, rumours and social pressure – with Regency costumes, live vocals and aerial acrobatics. If you love the atmosphere of Bridgerton, you will feel right at home.

Do I need to have seen Bridgerton to understand the show?

No. The story of Lady Arabella Fairmont is self-contained and can be enjoyed without any prior knowledge. Bridgerton fans will, however, discover plenty of nods to the setting, etiquette and musical style of the series.

Is Season of Desire a dinner show?

No. This Bridgerton-inspired production is a stage show, not a dinner package – your ticket covers the performance only. Food and drinks can be ordered separately at the Vindobona restaurant, which opens one hour before the show.

When are the performances?

There are three scheduled dates for the Bridgerton-inspired show:

  • Friday, 16 October 2026 – Premiere, 19:30
  • Friday, 22 January 2027 – 19:30
  • Friday, 19 February 2027 – 19:30
When do doors open and when does the show start?

Doors and the restaurant open at 18:30, and the performance starts at 19:30. We recommend arriving in good time – seats are allocated on arrival.

Where in Vienna does the Bridgerton-inspired show take place?

At Das Vindobona, Wallensteinplatz 6, 1200 Vienna.

Where can I get tickets for Season of Desire?

Tickets are available at vindobona.wien. The Vindobona box office is available on Wednesdays from 14:00 to 18:00 and on performance days (Monday–Saturday) from 17:30 to 19:00. You can also call +43 1 512 39 03 (extension 2).

What ticket categories are available?

Das Vindobona is a seated venue with four ticket categories:

  • KAT 1 – Premium tables near the front or in the boxes, for couples and small groups
  • KAT 2 – Larger shared tables
  • KAT 3 – Tables for two at the back, row 1
  • KAT 4 – Tables for two at the back, rows 2–3

Your specific seats within the chosen category are allocated on arrival.

Can I have something to eat before the show?

Yes. The restaurant at Vindobona opens one hour before the show. Orders placed by 19:00 will be served before the performance – anything ordered later will be brought to your table during the interval.

Is there an interval?

Yes, the performance includes an interval. Restaurant orders placed later in the evening are also served during this time.

Who is Season of Desire for?

For anyone looking for an evening that brings together opera, variety theatre and pop: Bridgerton fans, girls’ nights out, couples, birthday groups and corporate parties looking for more than a concert. The show is performed in German, with some songs sung in their original language.
