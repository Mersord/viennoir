Santa Baby · FAQ

Common questions about the festive dinner show Santa Baby

General

What is “Santa Baby” about?

Santa is looking for a wife. His elves Holly and Jolly therefore secretly hold auditions for the future Mrs Claus, while Ivy Grimm is determined to stop them. The result is an evening of live vocals, dance and comedy, accompanied by a festive three-course menu.

When and where does the show take place?

The show takes place at Das Vindobona in Vienna (Wallensteinplatz 6, 1200 Vienna) on:

  • Friday, 27 November 2026
  • Saturday, 28 November 2026
  • Wednesday, 9 December 2026
  • Thursday, 10 December 2026
What is the schedule and how long does the evening last?

The evening runs from 18:30 until approximately 21:30:

  • 18:30 Doors open
  • 19:00 Starter
  • 19:30 Show begins
  • 20:00 Main course and interval
  • 20:45 Dessert and interval
  • Approx. 21:30 End

The courses are served between the show segments, so you will not miss any of the performance.

When should we arrive?

We recommend arriving when doors open at 18:30. This leaves enough time for the cloakroom and finding your seats before the starter is served at 19:00.

What language is the show performed in?

In German and English, making the show suitable for international teams and guests as well.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes. The show is for guests aged 18 and over. Santa Baby is a festive story for adults.

Tickets and booking

Where can I buy tickets?

Tickets are available online through Das Vindobona at vindobona.wien. You can also find the link for each date above on this page.

What is included in the ticket?

The show, an amuse-bouche and the three-course menu. Drinks are not included.

What is the difference between the ticket categories?
  • CAT 1: Premium tables for couples or private groups in the front section, the upper front row or a box
  • CAT 2: Larger shared tables
  • CAT 3: Tables for two at the back, row 1
  • CAT 4: Tables for two at the back, rows 2 and 3

The seating plan on this page shows the exact table locations.

Do the tickets make a good gift?

Yes, they make an excellent gift. Tickets are transferable and can be purchased directly in the recipient’s name.

Can I cancel my tickets?

No, cancellations are not possible. If you cannot attend on your chosen date, you may pass the ticket on to someone else because tickets are transferable.

Food and drinks

What will be served?
  • Amuse-bouche: Smoked salmon rolls on leaf salad
  • Starter: Cream of chestnut soup with truffled croutons
  • Main course: Braised beef cheeks in red-wine sauce with potato gratin, honey-glazed carrots and Brussels sprouts
  • Dessert: Chocolate lava cake with vanilla sauce and festive berry compote
Are vegetarian and vegan dishes available? What about allergies?

The vegetarian main course is porcini risotto with Parmesan and king oyster mushrooms. A vegan menu is available on request. Please notify us of vegan menu requests, allergies and intolerances at least five days before the event by emailing office@viennoir.com.

How do drinks work?

You order drinks directly at your table and pay for them at the end of the evening.

Companies and groups

Can we hold our company Christmas party at Santa Baby?

Yes, of course. Dinner, entertainment and a festive setting are all organised in one evening, so your team does not have to plan them separately. For corporate bookings, email events@viennoir.com.

Will our group be seated together?

Yes. CAT 1 premium tables are also intended for private groups. For larger teams, we can arrange several tables together by prior agreement. Please book early, as places in December are limited.

Can we receive a company invoice?

Yes. An invoice made out to your company is available. Simply provide your company details when booking.

When must we confirm the final number of guests?

No later than ten days before the event.

Can we book the show exclusively for our company?

For private performances, additional dates or bespoke packages, please contact our events team at events@viennoir.com.

At the venue

How do I get there? Is parking available?
  • Public transport: Take tram 5 or 33 to Wallensteinplatz, or the U4 underground to Friedensbrücke followed by a short walk.
  • By car: Garage Brigittaplatz is around 350 metres from Das Vindobona. Parking costs from €2.00 per hour or part thereof.
Is the venue accessible?

Yes, Das Vindobona is accessible. If you require a particular place, for example for a wheelchair, please let us know when booking.

Is there a dress code?

There is no formal dress code. Festive attire suits the evening beautifully, however, and a touch of sparkle is expressly welcome.
