The story

Festive matchmaking with consequences.

Santa is single. His elves think that has to change.

Holly and Jolly secretly launch auditions for the future Mrs Claus. Candidates arrive, expectations become tangled and Ivy Grimm does everything she can to stop the romantic Christmas project.

Santa Baby combines dinner show, live music, dance and sharp comedy in a festive story for adults. Opulent, playful and guaranteed to be anything but a silent night.
